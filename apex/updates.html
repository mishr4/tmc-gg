Campaign July 2026

The quiet phase begins

Something lovely is happening before we’ve asked anyone for anything. The campaign hasn’t launched publicly — the thermometer on our Progress page still reads $0 — and yet the first pledge conversations have already started. Neighbors are writing to us first.

To our early believers: thank you. You’re proof that this center isn’t a pitch, it’s a plan people were already waiting for. Quiet-phase gifts and pledges are what let us walk into site conversations with real momentum at our back — a number on the board before the board even goes up.

What happens next: we keep gathering pledges gently through the fall, our advisors narrow the site shortlist, and when the moment is right we open the campaign to everyone — still aiming to open doors in 2027. If you’d like your name in that very first chapter, we’d be honored.

We spent June eating our way through the neighborhood — fourteen potluck dinners, hundreds of conversations. What neighbors asked for most, in order: a real kitchen, a dance floor that can take a beating, and one quiet room for prayer, homework, and deep breaths.

Site May 2026

What we’re looking for in a home

Our wish list, honestly ranked: close to a bus line, one hall big enough for a wedding or a hundred folding chairs, a kitchen that can feed a crowd, and daylight — as much of it as we can get. If that building exists, we’ll find it.

Campaign March 2026

Why a permanent home?

Renting rooms by the hour means packing your culture into a tote bag at 9pm sharp. Owning a home means the drums stay set up, the recipes live in the kitchen, and nobody asks permission to belong. That’s the whole case, really — $2.4M to root it forever.
