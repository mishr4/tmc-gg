Where the money goes

Every dollar has a job.

No mystery line items, no vague “operations” bucket. Four numbers that add up to exactly $2.4M.

Site & building

$1.6M67%

A permanent home — the land and the walls that make everything else possible.

Renovation & accessibility

$450K19%

Ramps, kitchens, quiet rooms, and good acoustics, so every body and every tradition fits comfortably.

First-year programs

$250K10%

Classes, celebrations, and staff, so the center feels alive from the very first week.

Operating reserve

$100K4%

A rainy-day cushion, so the lights stay on even when fundraising has a slow season.

$1.6M + $450K + $250K + $100K = $2.4M, to the dollar.

Milestones

The road to opening day.

Six honest steps between today and the party. We’ll mark them off right here as they happen.

  1. Quiet-phase gifts & pledges

    Now

    Where we are today — early gifts and pledges from first believers set the pace for everything that follows.

  2. Confirm site & community partners

    Next

    Locking in the right building in the right neighborhood, alongside the partners who’ll bring it to life.

  3. Design & permits

    Later

    Turning community wishlists into floor plans — and floor plans into stamped, approved drawings.

  4. Renovation & build-out

    Later

    Walls, ramps, kitchens, classrooms. The dusty, noisy, wonderful part.

  5. Soft opening with first programs

    Later · aiming 2027

    First classes, first celebrations, first cups of tea in a home that finally exists.

  6. Grand opening celebration

    Later

    The big one. 40+ communities, one very good day. You’re already invited.

Honest FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

The things people actually ask us — answered the way we’d answer a friend.

Honest answer: our 501(c)(3) application is pending with the IRS. If it’s approved — and we expect it will be — recognition is typically retroactive to our filing date, so gifts made now should qualify. We’ll send proper receipts either way, and if anything changes we’ll tell you straight. If timing matters for your taxes, email hello@apexmcc.org and we’ll walk you through exactly where things stand.

The whole budget, in one breath: $1.6M secures the site and building, $450K makes it accessible and welcoming, $250K funds a full first year of programs, and $100K sits in reserve so we never open the doors on an empty tank. That’s it — every dollar accounted for, no mystery line items.

We’re aiming to open doors in 2027. Full honesty: that depends on how the quiet phase goes, how the site search lands, and how kind the permitting process is. We’d rather open a little later than open unready — and we’ll post every twist on the Updates page as it happens.
