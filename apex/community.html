Our community

One home, forty-plus ways to belong.

Every week, neighbors across the region host traditions, teach languages, and share resources — in borrowed rooms, church basements, and parking garages. Apex is the permanent home they’re building together, aiming to open its doors in 2027.

Communities

Who’s already at the table.

Twelve of the forty-plus groups shaping Apex — each with a tradition to host, a story to tell, and a very strong opinion about snacks.

Showing all 12 communities — and counting.

FC

Filipino Cultural Circle

Folk dance rehearsals and kamayan nights where the table is one long banana leaf.

EE

Ethiopian & Eritrean Families

Coffee ceremonies, slow and fragrant — three rounds, no rushing anyone.

VE

Vietnamese Elders Club

Tea, chess, and stories that deserve a much bigger audience.

PA

Punjabi Arts Collective

Bhangra practice and visual arts, with the volume set to joyful.

UN

Ukrainian Neighbors

Crafts, pysanky, and holiday markets that smell like home.

FS

Folklórico de la Sierra

A dance troupe with swirling skirts and big dreams of a real floor.

SY

Somali Youth Alliance

Mentorship and sports for the next generation of neighbors.

KC

Korean Culture School

Weekend language classes where grandparents are the guest stars.

PI

Pacific Islander Fellowship

Family days, song circles, and aunties who make sure everyone eats twice.

CR

Capoeira Roda Aberta

Movement, music, and a circle that’s always open to one more.

AS

Arab American Storytellers

Poetry nights and oral history, passed carefully hand to hand.

NV

Native Voices Language Table

Language revitalization, one shared meal and one lesson at a time.
