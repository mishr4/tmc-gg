Cookie Policy

Last updated 30 June 2026

This page explains how tmc.gg uses cookies and similar browser storage. In short: we use only what's essential to run the site and remember your choices. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

What cookies are

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data a website can save in your browser. They let a site remember things between visits — for example, whether you've already dismissed a notice.

What we use

  • Consent preference (essential). When you accept or decline our cookie notice, we save that choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Security & delivery (essential). Our hosting and content-delivery provider may set strictly necessary cookies to keep the site secure and served efficiently.

We do not currently use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies. If that ever changes, we'll update this page and ask for your consent first.

Third-party content

Some pages load resources from third parties — Google Fonts for typography, and embedded links to Discord and YouTube. These providers may set their own cookies when their content loads or when you follow those links, under their own policies.

Managing your choices

You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which will bring back the cookie notice on your next visit. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as intended.

More information

For the bigger picture on how we handle data, see our Privacy Policy. Questions? Email tagnz@news.tmc.gg.
