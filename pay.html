Protocol.

Zero Card Custody

TMC Pay never sees, stores, or transmits your card number. When you press pay, you are handed directly to Stripe — the same PCI-DSS Level 1 processor used by Amazon and Google. We receive a confirmation, nothing more.

Protocol: Delegated Trust

Refunds & Disputes

Wrong amount? Duplicate charge? Write to us within 14 days with your receipt and we reverse it — no forms, no fights. Disputes are answered by a human within two business days.

Protocol: Human Resolution

Zero Retention

Your email is used once — to send the receipt. It is not added to a list, sold, or profiled. The ledger records the reference, the amount, and the timestamp. That is the entire record.

Protocol: Zero Footprint

Need a Hand?

Lost your invoice reference, need a custom amount above the limit, or want to pay by another method? The support desk can generate a private checkout link for any arrangement.
