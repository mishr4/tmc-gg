What is the RBSC?

Eight broadcasters. One stage in Zürich. One winner — decided by you.

The Robroadcasting Song Contest is Mavion News' music event of the year. Each country sends an artist and an original song, the continent tunes in live, and a public vote sends one of them to the top of the scoreboard. Watch every performance, back your favourites, and follow the points all the way to the gold douze points.

01 · The format

Two semi-finals, one Grand Final

Semi-finals run Oct 10 and 11; the survivors meet in the Grand Final on Oct 17, live from the Aurora Arena in Zürich.

02 · The entries

Real broadcasters, original songs

Every entry is sent by a real Roblox broadcaster — an act and an original song competing for their country, Eurovision-style.

03 · The vote

The public decides

Voting is free and runs during the live shows, audited with SRB Network. The leader takes the gold douze points.
