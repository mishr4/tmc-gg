What is the RBSC?

Eight broadcasters. One stage in Zürich. One winner — decided by you.

The Robroadcasting Song Contest is Cirya Noticias' music event of the year. Each country sends an artist and an original song, the continent tunes in live, and a public vote sends one of them to the top of the scoreboard. Watch every performance, back your favourites, and follow the points all the way to the gold douze points.
