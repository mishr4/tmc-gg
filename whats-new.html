What's improved

Polished where it counts.

Redesigned navigation

A clean sticky corporate header with clear active states and a blur that settles in as you scroll.

Better performance

Static pages, a single small stylesheet and almost no JavaScript — no frameworks, no trackers. Pages render fast and feel instant.

Improved accessibility

Semantic landmarks, visible focus rings, skip links, real contrast and full keyboard support — including the new menus.

A new editorial identity

A near-black canvas, a serif voice in Fraunces, layered surfaces, and colour used only where it means something — blue for interactive, red for live.

Better mobile experience

Mobile-first layouts that stack cleanly, a collapsing menu, tap-friendly buttons and nothing that overflows or crowds.

Enhanced reading experience

Comfortable line lengths, a clear type hierarchy and generous spacing make every page easier to read and scan.
