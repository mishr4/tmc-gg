Questions

Frequently asked.

What is TMCast?
TMCast is TMC's broadcast platform. It powers our internal radio operations and is available for public hosting — anyone can run their own internet radio station on our infrastructure.
Do I need ASCAP licensing?
If you plan to stream copyrighted music, yes — you'll need an ASCAP (or equivalent) license. TMCast hosting does not include music licensing. We offer an ASCAP licensing service through mavion.tmc.gg/licensing to make it easy.
Can I go live with a microphone or DJ software?
Yes. Pro and Business plans support live DJ connections via standard broadcasting software like BUTT, Mixxx or SAM Broadcaster. You get dedicated mount points for seamless handoff between AutoDJ and live.
How do I get started?
Join our Discord, pick a plan, and we'll have your station set up and streaming within 24 hours. You'll get access to the full TMCast dashboard to manage your station.
Can I use a custom domain?
Pro and Business plans support custom domains. Point your domain (e.g. radio.yourdomain.com) to your TMCast station and we'll configure SSL automatically.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — all plans are month-to-month with no contracts. Cancel anytime and your station stays active until the end of your billing period.
