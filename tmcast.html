Step 2 · Choose how it plays

Pick your automation depth.

Five priority layers, top to bottom. The highest layer with audio wins. Use one, use all, mix freely — TMCast handles the fallback for you.

Live inputs · optional · use one, both, or neither

Running a fully automated station? Skip this whole section — AutoDJ handles 24/7 on its own.

Priority1

Guest DJ input

A second live slot so a co-host or invited DJ can take over for an hour without kicking you offline. When they disconnect, your Studio feed is right there waiting.

Priority2

Studio input — your encoder

Broadcast live from your own software — SAM Broadcaster, BUTT, Mixxx, RadioBOSS, Virtual DJ, OBS, or any Icecast/Shoutcast source. Keep the workflow you know. On air the moment you connect.

AutoDJ · each layer falls back to the next

Priority3

Scheduled playlists

For the programmer. Run specific shows at specific hours — morning show, drivetime, weekend specials. You don't need to fill the whole week: empty slots fall through to rotation automatically, so partial schedules just work.

Priority4

Weighted rotation

For the CentovaCast veteran. The familiar rotation model — A-list, B-list, jingles, sweepers, each with a weight. More control than random, without the work of a full schedule.

Priority5

Random library

Upload and be live. AutoDJ shuffles your whole library forever — perfect as a simple music-only station, or as your emergency fallback when the studio drops and nothing is scheduled. You never go dark.

Smart fallback, built in. The highest layer with audio wins. No live show? The schedule plays. Empty schedule slot? Rotation plays. Empty rotation? Your library plays. You never need to fill every hour manually.

Step 3 · Go live · optional

Keep your studio software.

Already broadcasting with SAM Broadcaster, BUTT, Mixxx, RadioBOSS, Virtual DJ, OBS or a hardware encoder? Keep using it. Point it at your Studio input, hit connect, and you're live — AutoDJ steps aside. Disconnect and AutoDJ resumes exactly where the schedule left off.

Hand off without a beat of silence.

Need a co-host for an hour? They connect to the separate Guest input. Your Studio stream keeps running underneath, so when they hop off you're right back on air — no dead air, no reconnects.

Running fully automated?

Skip this step entirely — most of our stations do. Point any Icecast- or Shoutcast-compatible encoder at your TMCast Studio input when you want it; the only thing that changes is the server address.

How we're different

Fewer constraints than the incumbents.

vs schedule-first platforms

No forced scheduling.

Some platforms push you toward scheduled playlists for every hour of the week. With TMCast, scheduling is one option among three — empty slots fall through to rotation or library automatically.

vs CentovaCast / SonicPanel

Same rotation model, modern panel.

If you came from CentovaCast or SonicPanel, the weighted rotation feels familiar. TMCast Studio is our own panel, built from scratch — no PHP cron jobs, no licensing fees, and an HLS CDN included so mobile listeners stop buffering.

vs self-hosting

Unlimited listeners, flat price.

No per-listener bills, no Liquidsoap config, no TLS renewals at 3am. Self-hosted setups drift out of sync, run out of disk, or quietly stop transcoding on an update. We monitor and patch all of that for you.

What it looks like in the real world.

Working DJ · SAM Broadcaster

Live at 8pm, automated by midnight.

Connect SAM at 8pm for your live show — AutoDJ pauses, your stream takes over. At 10pm you disconnect and the scheduled "Late Night Chill" playlist resumes. At midnight the schedule ends and weighted rotation carries on till morning.

Cafe · automation only

Upload once, walk away.

Upload 800 tracks of acoustic chill. No schedule, no rotation, no live input — random library plays forever. The cafe just needs a URL on the speakers. Zero ongoing work.

24/7 studio · outage

Your studio is always live. Then the ISP drops for ten minutes. AutoDJ kicks in instantly, listeners stay connected and keep hearing music, and when your studio comes back the live feed fades in smoothly. No reconnects, no listeners lost.

Community station · guest DJs

Volunteers on, no dead air.

Your studio runs the house mix 24/7. When a volunteer wants a guest show, they connect to the Guest input and take over. When they disconnect, your studio feed comes right back — never a second of silence.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What is TMCast?
TMCast is TMC's broadcast platform. It powers our internal radio operations and is available for public hosting — anyone can run their own internet radio station on our infrastructure.
Do I need ASCAP licensing?
If you plan to stream copyrighted music, yes — you'll need an ASCAP (or equivalent) license. TMCast hosting does not include music licensing. We offer an ASCAP licensing service through mavion.tmc.gg/licensing to make it easy.
Can I go live with a microphone or DJ software?
Yes. Pro and Business plans support live DJ connections via standard broadcasting software like BUTT, Mixxx or SAM Broadcaster. You get dedicated mount points for seamless handoff between AutoDJ and live.
How do the five priority layers work?
Guest DJ input (1), Studio input (2), scheduled playlists (3), weighted rotation (4) and random library (5). The highest layer with audio playing wins, and each falls back to the next automatically — so a partial schedule, or a dropped live feed, never leaves you with dead air. Use one layer or all five.
What audio formats can I upload?
MP3, WAV, FLAC and OGG. We read the ID3 tags, cache the cover art, and run loudness normalization on upload so tracks sit at a consistent volume. Reuse your existing library — there's no re-tagging.
Can I embed the player on my own site?
Yes, on every plan. Drop the responsive embeddable player (six themes, live album art and now-playing) or individual widgets — now-playing, listener count, recent history, stream status — onto any page with a copy-paste snippet. You also get HLS, Icecast MP3, and M3U/PLS URLs for VLC, TuneIn, Radio Garden and other directories.
How do I get started?
Join our Discord, pick a plan, and we'll have your station set up and streaming within 24 hours. You'll get access to the full TMCast dashboard to manage your station.
Can I use a custom domain?
Yes — Pro and Business plans support custom domains. Custom domains need a quick consultation first so we can set up the IPs and redirects on our side. It's fairly easy: about 5–10 minutes of setup, then 1–2 days for everything to be fully live and propagated. Point your domain (e.g. radio.yourdomain.com) at your TMCast station and we'll configure SSL automatically.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — all plans are month-to-month with no contracts. Cancel anytime and your station stays active until the end of your billing period.
