How an engagement runs

NDC / 02 — Formats
Format A

The Sprint

1–2 weeks
  • Brand audit of everything you currently ship
  • One working session with your team
  • A short, blunt findings memo
  • A prioritized fix list your volunteers can execute

Best for: orgs that suspect the problem but can't name it yet.

Format B

The Identity Build

4–8 weeks
  • Discovery with staff, board and the people you serve
  • Naming (if needed), mark, type and color system
  • Guidelines written for non-designers
  • Templates in the tools your team already uses

Best for: new orgs, renames, and brands that outgrew the founder's Canva file.

Format C

The Site Build

3–6 weeks · select
  • Content-first structure — write it, then design it
  • Static build: fast, secure, no tracking
  • Donation / volunteer / program pages that convert
  • A one-hour hand-off training, recorded

Best for: orgs whose identity is ready but whose site is losing them trust.

Fair questions

NDC / 05 — FAQ
Q1

What does it cost?

It scales with the size of the organization, not the size of our ambition. Sprints are priced so small orgs can say yes; identity builds are quoted after a first call, in writing, with nothing that grows later. If money is the blocker, say so in the first email — sometimes we can shape the scope until it fits.

Q2

Do you only work with nonprofits?

Mostly. Nonprofits, community organizations, mutual-aid groups, youth programs, volunteer media — if the mission is the point, we're interested. If you're a company that wants nonprofit pricing, we'll politely point you elsewhere.

Q3

Who owns the work?

You do. Every file, every source, every font license, documented at hand-off. We build brands your team can run without calling us — if you need us forever, we did it wrong.

Q4

How do we start?

Email us three things: what your organization does, what's not working, and roughly when you need it. No decks, no forms, no discovery-call gauntlet. We'll reply with an honest read on whether we're the right fit.
