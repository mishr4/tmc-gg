What we do

NDC / 01 — Services
01

Brand identity

Names, marks, type, color — an identity system your team can actually run after we're gone. Guidelines included, jargon not.

02

Brand consultation

A working session or a short engagement. We tell you what to fix, what to leave alone, and what not to spend money on.

03

Websites* Select engagements

Design and build, selectively — when a site is what the brand actually needs.

* We take on a small number of web builds each year — only when we're genuinely the right fit. We'd rather say no than ship something we're not proud of.

How we work

NDC / 03 — Approach
01

Listen

We start with your people, your funders, and what the organization actually needs said about it.

02

Distill

One page: who you are, who it's for, why it matters. If we can't write it, we can't design it.

03

Design

Identity, system, guidelines — built for nonprofit-real budgets and the tools your team already uses.

04

Hand off

You keep every file. Your team runs the brand without us — that's the point.
