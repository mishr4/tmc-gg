About

A media company that thinks like an engineering team.

The Mavion Corporation is a California-based media and technology corporation. We build high-fidelity audio networks, broadcast platforms and the global infrastructure that carries them.

Who we are

Built in California, running worldwide.

TMC owns and operates Mavion, TMCast, Mavion MServices, Inline Television and RHL Now (formerly AsteraPlay) — building decentralized audio networks, television content and global server infrastructure.

We believe data privacy is a right, not a privilege. Every piece of infrastructure we build runs zero-retention protocols — we refuse to track or sell user data.
