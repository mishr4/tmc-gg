Corporate overview.

The Mavion Corporation is a privately held media and technology parent company based in California. This page summarizes how the company is structured, who leads it, and the standards it operates by.

Profile

Company at a glance.

Mavion is organized as a holding company that owns and operates a group of media brands and the technology that distributes them. The corporation provides shared infrastructure, engineering and editorial support to its subsidiaries, which run their own day-to-day operations.

Operating standards

How we operate.

Privacy by default

Every system the corporation runs uses zero-retention protocols. Mavion does not track users or sell audience data, across its own brands and the broadcasters it hosts.

Reliability

The group's broadcast network spans seven server clusters across four continents, so a single station carries the reliability of the whole company.

Shared support

Engineering and editorial teams operate at the corporate level, letting small subsidiaries work to a consistent standard without building it alone.
