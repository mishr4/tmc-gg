# Mavion Homepage Product Design Brief

Redesign and maintain the Mavion homepage as a premium interactive product experience while preserving the established dark editorial identity.

## Identity

- Keep the existing Mavion navigation and near-black visual system.
- Use Fraunces for expressive headlines with balanced wrapping and comfortable line spacing.
- Use Inter for interface text, controls, labels, and body copy.
- Use blue as the primary interactive accent.
- Avoid fake metrics, generic testimonials, stock imagery, and unsupported claims.

## Landing flow

1. A focused hero that explains Mavion immediately.
2. A functional product preview rather than a decorative mockup.
3. An interactive 3D network globe tied to pointer and scroll movement.
4. A card-based scroll story connecting creation, broadcasting, and community.
5. Live interface demonstrations for Newsroom, TMCast, Directory, and Checkout.
6. Audience-specific pathways for publishers, broadcasters, organizations, and learners.
7. A direct product launchpad with real destinations.
8. Clear company, team, announcement, FAQ, and contact sections.

## Interactions

- Smooth anchor scrolling and scroll reveal transitions.
- Pointer-reactive depth with restrained tilt and lighting.
- Sticky cards that stack and transform through the page.
- Editable local newsroom demo with a publish state.
- Working TMCast transport and queue preview.
- Keyboard-accessible product tabs.
- Global command search using Ctrl K or Command K.
- Floating section navigation and page progress.
- Reduced-motion fallbacks for every major animation.

## Quality requirements

- Every button must perform an understandable action.
- Every product name must link to its real destination when one exists.
- Mobile layouts must remain readable and usable without horizontal page overflow.
- Decorative 3D elements must never cover text or controls.
- Focus states, semantic labels, and keyboard operation are required.
- Copy should be concise, specific, and grounded in real Mavion products.
- New sections must feel connected to the page rather than added as unrelated cards.
