Companies

The companies and operations of Mavion.

Mavion is organized as a parent company. Three subsidiaries form the core of the group, supported by a set of audio, entertainment and video divisions that share the corporation's infrastructure.

Subsidiaries

The core of the group.

Mavion News

Flagship operationMedia & news network

The corporation's flagship media operation, combining a 24/7 radio network with a growing news platform. Mavion News produces music, live programming and reporting, and is the audience the rest of the group builds toward.

Its programming is delivered on TMCast infrastructure, and its newsroom is expanding through UnoNoticias.

Visit Mavion News →

TMCast

Public hosting liveBroadcast technology

The group's broadcast-technology arm. TMCast hosts, schedules and distributes audio for Mavion's stations, running on a network of server clusters across four continents with zero-retention handling of listener data.

It is also offered to independent broadcasters outside the group — the corporation's only subsidiary sold as a service. Managed plans start at $10 per month.

About TMCast →

UnoNoticias

In development · 2026Newsroom

A dedicated newsroom under the Mavion Media Network, currently building a headquarters and studio for original news coverage — an editorial floor, live broadcast sets and a newsroom.

It broadens the corporation's journalism beyond Mavion News and broadcasts through TMCast. Completion is expected in July 2026.

Shared infrastructure

One network beneath every brand.

Every company in the group runs on the same foundation — a broadcast network of seven server clusters across four continents, operated with zero-retention protocols. It is the reason a small newsroom or station can broadcast with enterprise reliability.
