Who we are

A parent company for independent media.

The Mavion Corporation is a privately held media and technology company headquartered in California. It owns and operates a group of media brands and the technology that carries them — from newsrooms and radio networks to the broadcast platform that distributes them.

Rather than a single product, Mavion is organized as a holding company. Each subsidiary runs its own operation and audience, while infrastructure, engineering, and editorial standards are maintained at the corporate level — so a small newsroom or station can operate with the reliability of a much larger organization.

Our mission

Give independent media the tools that scale.

Mavion exists so that small and independent media organizations can operate with the infrastructure, technology, and reach usually reserved for far larger companies. The corporation invests in broadcast systems, publishing platforms, and newsrooms — and holds each to the same standard of reliability and privacy, including zero-retention handling of audience data.

Mavion News

Flagship operationMedia & news network

Mavion's flagship media operation. Mavion News produces and broadcasts music, live programming and news directly to listeners and readers, anchoring the group's relationship with its audience.

It is the hub the rest of the corporation builds toward — programming reaches audiences through TMCast, and its journalism is expanding through UnoNoticias.

Visit Mavion News →

TMCast

Public hosting liveBroadcast technology

The corporation's broadcast-technology arm. TMCast is the platform that hosts, schedules and distributes audio for Mavion's stations, and is offered to independent broadcasters outside the group.

It is the infrastructure layer beneath every Mavion broadcast — and the only subsidiary offered as a service to third parties.

UnoNoticias

In development · 2026Newsroom

A dedicated newsroom operating under the Mavion Media Network. UnoNoticias is building a headquarters and studio to write, anchor and broadcast original news coverage.

It extends the corporation's journalism beyond Mavion News, sharing editorial resources and broadcasting through TMCast. Completion is expected in 2026.
