Frequently Asked Questions

What is CiryaCast?
CiryaCast is TMC's custom broadcast platform built on AzuraCast. It powers all of TMC's internal radio operations, and we also offer public hosting so anyone can run their own internet radio station on our infrastructure.
Do I need ASCAP licensing?
If you plan to stream copyrighted music, yes — you'll need an ASCAP (or equivalent) license. CiryaCast hosting does not include music licensing. We offer an ASCAP licensing service through cirya.co/licensing to make it easy.
Can I go live with a microphone or DJ software?
Yes! Pro and Business plans support live DJ connections via standard broadcasting software (like BUTT, Mixxx, or SAM Broadcaster). You get dedicated mount points for seamless handoff between AutoDJ and live.
How do I get started?
Join our Discord, pick a plan, and we'll have your station set up and streaming within 24 hours. You'll get access to the full CiryaCast dashboard to manage your station.
Can I use a custom domain?
Pro and Business plans support custom domains. Point your domain (e.g., radio.yourdomain.com) to your CiryaCast station and we'll configure SSL automatically.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, all plans are month-to-month with no contracts. Cancel anytime and your station will remain active until the end of your billing period.

Who We Are

The Mishra Corporation is a California-based media and technology corporation. TMC owns and operates Cirya, CiryaCast, Cirya MServices, Inline Television, and RHL Now (formerly AsteraPlay) — building high-fidelity decentralized audio networks, television content, and global server infrastructure.

We believe that data privacy is a right, not a privilege. That's why every piece of infrastructure we build operates with zero-retention protocols — we refuse to track or sell user data.
