Who We Are

The Mishra Corporation is a California-based media and technology corporation. TMC owns and operates Cirya, CiryaCast, Cirya MServices, and RHL Now (formerly AsteraPlay) — building high-fidelity decentralized audio networks and global server infrastructure.

We believe that data privacy is a right, not a privilege. That's why every piece of infrastructure we build operates with zero-retention protocols — we refuse to track or sell user data.
