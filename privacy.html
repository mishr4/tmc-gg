Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 June 2026

This policy explains how The Mavion Corporation ("Mavion," "we," "us") handles information when you visit tmc.gg. We build our services to collect as little personal data as possible, and we do not sell it.

Information we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum:

  • Preferences. Your cookie choice is stored in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again. It never leaves your device.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us or contact us on Discord, we receive whatever you choose to share (such as your name and email) so we can reply.
  • Basic request logs. Like any website, our hosting provider records standard technical information such as IP address and browser type to keep the service secure and reliable.

We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking, and we do not build behavioural profiles of visitors.

How we use information

We use the limited information above to operate and secure the site, respond to enquiries, review job applications, and understand aggregate, non-identifying usage so we can improve. We rely on your consent for non-essential cookies (currently none) and our legitimate interest in running a secure website for everything else.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies and local storage. For detail, see our Cookie Policy.

Third-party services

Some parts of the site rely on trusted third parties, which receive only what's needed to function:

  • Google Fonts serves our typefaces; your browser requests them from Google.
  • Our careers listing loads open roles from a Mavion API on mavion.tmc.gg.
  • Discord and YouTube host our community and live broadcasts; visiting those links is subject to their own policies.

Data retention

We operate on a zero-retention principle: we don't store what we don't need. Correspondence is kept only as long as necessary to handle your request, and technical logs are held briefly for security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email tagnz@news.tmc.gg and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Your California privacy rights

The Mavion Corporation is based in California. If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), gives you specific rights over your personal information:

  • Right to know. Request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the purpose, and any parties we share it with.
  • Right to delete. Request that we delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to correct. Ask us to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. Direct us not to sell or share your personal information.
  • Right to limit sensitive information. Limit our use of sensitive personal information to what's necessary to provide the service.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months. We also do not use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond the purposes permitted by law. Because there is nothing to opt out of, we don't offer a separate "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" mechanism — but you may still contact us with any request.

Categories collected. In the last 12 months we may have collected "identifiers" (such as an email address, if you contact us) and "internet or network activity" (standard technical logs). We collect these to operate and secure the site and to respond to you — not to build advertising profiles.

How to exercise your rights. Email tagnz@news.tmc.gg with "California privacy request" in the subject. We will verify your request and respond within the timeframes required by law. You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

California's "Shine the Light" law (Civil Code § 1798.83) also lets California residents ask about disclosures of personal information to third parties for their direct-marketing purposes. We do not disclose personal information for third-party direct marketing.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email tagnz@news.tmc.gg. The Mavion Corporation is based in California, USA.
