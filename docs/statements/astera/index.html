Public statement

Response to Astera Group’s restriction notice

We acknowledge the public notice dated 20 July 2026 concerning Mavion, its related projects, and Alex Mishra.

Published 22 July 2026

Our position

Mavion does not accept the allegations in the notice as an established account of events. We are reviewing the underlying claims and our records. We will not use this statement to target Astera, its staff, or its community.

We will respect the stated boundary

Astera has said it does not want contact from Mavion or its associated projects. We will respect that direction. We do not encourage staff, members, or community participants to contact Astera on Mavion’s behalf.

An allegation is not an admission

The notice contains allegations concerning assets, confidential information, and policy breaches. We do not treat those allegations as findings of fact, and nothing in this response is an admission of wrongdoing.

Asset concerns should be specific

We have removed material when a rights holder identified it and requested removal. If Astera believes a current Mavion asset infringes its rights, a specific report with the original source and the exact material at issue allows us to review it responsibly.

We are improving our process

Our current standard is to use original or clearly licensed assets, retain source records for shared material, and handle valid removal requests promptly. Historical work is being reviewed against that standard.

What happens next

We will continue operating independently and focus on our own work. We ask our community to do the same: do not harass, contact, or speculate about people connected to Astera. If a genuine legal or rights issue is raised through an appropriate channel, we will respond through that channel.

Rights concern

For a documented rights concern relating to Mavion material, contact tagnz@tmc.gg with the original source and the relevant Mavion URL or asset. This is a statement of our position, not legal advice.
