Our response to community concerns

A clear account of questions raised about Mavion, Cirya, historical assets, and community activity in Roblox-related spaces.

Published 22 July 2026Read time: 4 minutes

On this page

PurposeClarificationsOur standardsContact

Why we are publishing this

We have seen posts and articles that raise concerns about our past work, our use of assets, and the conduct of people in our community. We take legitimate concerns seriously. This page is our direct response, written to be specific without escalating personal disputes.

Our position: Mavion does not endorse harassment, account compromise, asset theft, or hostile campaigns against other organizations. Where material did not meet our standards or a rights holder asked us to remove it, we removed it and improved our process.

Clarifications

Editorial images and commentary

Some images referenced in reporting were used as contextual commentary about public discussion, not as a claim of ownership over another organization’s work. We understand that visual context can still create confusion. We will remove or revise material when a valid rights concern is raised.

Free models and historical assets

A studio referenced in prior discussion was obtained as a free model. Our developers used limited elements from it. It was removed after a request. In other cases, assets were used during collaborations or while an experience was active, and were later removed once a request or change in permissions was communicated.

Nonprofit and educational material

Artwork and examples that referenced nonprofit or youth-oriented initiatives were intended as examples and test material. They were not presented as an endorsement, partnership, or representation of those organizations.

Testing content

Some videos, thumbnails, ident tests, ratings, and OBS material were created as experimental or collaborative media. They were not presented as operational broadcasts or official statements by third parties.

Security incidents

We do not support unauthorized access to radio stations, accounts, servers, or other systems. We are not responsible for actions taken by individuals outside our authority. When we learn that content in an official community space is inappropriate, we remove it and review moderation action.

Design similarities

We recognize that creative work can look similar, particularly with common broadcast conventions such as studios, displays, camera equipment, and 3D text. Similarity is not automatically copying. At the same time, we take direct asset and design concerns seriously and will review specific, documented claims in good faith.

Former partnerships and acquisitions

Past partnerships and acquired projects can create confusion about what material was authorized at a particular time. We are improving our records so that assets from a collaboration are removed or replaced promptly when the relationship or permission changes.

What we are doing now

We are focused on original, functional experiences and a healthier creative process. That means clearer asset records, documented permissions, faster takedown handling, and a higher bar for what appears in official projects and community channels.

  • We will review credible ownership concerns with the relevant material and context.
  • We will remove or replace material when rights are unclear or a valid request is made.
  • We will not use official channels to encourage harassment or personal attacks.
  • We will distinguish opinion, commentary, and verified reporting more clearly.

We cannot control every statement made by former members, partners, or unrelated community participants. We can control our own work, our official spaces, and how we respond. That is where our attention is going.

Questions or a rights concern?

If you believe we are using your work without permission, please send the original source, the relevant Mavion URL or asset, and your contact information to tagnz@tmc.gg. We will review it directly. This statement is provided for transparency and is not legal advice.
