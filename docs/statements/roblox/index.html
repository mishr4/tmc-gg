Public statement

A direct response to claims about Cirya

We made mistakes in earlier projects. We removed material when asked, tightened our process, and are focused on original work. Here are the facts behind the claims being repeated.

22 July 2026  |  Evidence supplied in our supporting record

Our position, plainly

We reject false, incomplete, or unsupported claims being presented as a final account of our work. We do not support asset theft, harassment, or unauthorized access to anyone’s systems. Where an owner identifies material and asks for removal, we remove it. Our focus is original, functional work and clearer standards going forward.

01
Claim: editorial image use

“Cirya used an RBC image to attack or impersonate RBC.”

Response: The image was used in editorial context alongside a screen discussing whether RBC was still good. It was not presented as Cirya’s asset or as an official RBC statement. We understand the use could still be read differently, and we will remove or revise imagery when a rights holder makes a valid request.

02
Claim: studio assets

“A studio or its assets were taken from another project.”

Response: The studio referenced was obtained as a free model. Our developers used limited elements, including microphones. When a removal request was made, the material was taken down. We now keep better records of asset sources and permissions.

03
Claim: broadcast and nonprofit material

“Testing content claimed to represent other organizations.”

Response: The material was test content and collaborative media work, including idents, ratings, thumbnails, and OBS demonstrations. References to nonprofit or youth-oriented initiatives were examples, not endorsements or official partnerships.

04
Claim: account or radio compromise

“Cirya was involved in unauthorized access to another organization’s systems.”

Response: We were not involved in any unauthorized access. We do not condone it. Access credentials and system security are the responsibility of the organization that issues and manages them. Any inappropriate community content that comes to our attention is removed and reviewed.

05
Claim: copied designs

“A sign, studio, or 3D text proves copying.”

Response: Common broadcast elements such as screens, cameras, studios, and 3D text are not exclusive to one group. Similarity alone is not proof of copying. Still, where a specific asset is identified and ownership is documented, we will investigate and remove or replace it if needed.

06
Claim: assets from a past partnership

“Collaboration assets stayed in use after a relationship changed.”

Response: Some material was supplied or used while collaborations were active. When permission changed or removal was requested, those assets were removed. We acknowledge that this should have been handled more quickly and with clearer documentation.

What is changing

  • New work uses original or clearly licensed assets.
  • We keep source and permission records for shared material.
  • We act on valid removal requests without turning them into public drama.
  • Official channels will not encourage harassment or personal attacks.

Contact us directly

If you believe we are using your work without permission, send the original source, the exact Mavion URL or asset, and your contact information to tagnz@tmc.gg. We will review it directly. This page is a statement of our position and is not legal advice.
